## CONTEXT

**⚠️ Updated on 19 July (day 4 added).** The 15:00 snapshot on 16 July recorded that no candidacy for defence minister had been submitted and the ministry sat empty; it also carried an earlier error, since corrected, that the president had already submitted Ihor Klymenko's candidacy. Both are now overtaken by events: on 17 July the Cabinet named an **acting** minister — not Klymenko but Yevhen Khmara.

What happened:

- **14.07.2026** — parliament accepted the resignation of Yuliia Svyrydenko's government. The entire cabinet, Fedorov included, became acting ministers.
- **15.07.2026** — Fedorov confirmed he was leaving the post, which he had held since 14.01.2026.
- **16.07.2026, from 09:00** — «cardboard protests» in 28 cities. The Kyiv action was called by veteran **Dmytro Koziatynskyi**, one of the organisers of the July 2025 protests in defence of NABU and SAPO.
- **16.07.2026, 12:12** — parliament elected **Serhii Koretskyi** prime minister, «За — **289**» (Yaroslav Zhelezniak; confirmed by LB at 12:13). The Defence and Foreign ministries were **deliberately excluded** from the cabinet vote.
- **17.07.2026** — protests ran for a **second day**. In Kyiv the evening crowd on Ivan Franko Square reached **«близько 3 тисяч людей»** (LB, own correspondent) and, by several accounts, **exceeded the first day** (Kyiv24: «зібрала значно більше людей, ніж у перший день акції»). Demands widened: besides Fedorov's return, protesters now called for the **dismissal of Commander-in-Chief Oleksandr Syrskyi** and the **recall of MPs** from a month-long recess. Zelensky publicly confirmed for the first time a **conflict between Syrskyi and Fedorov**, calling it a problem of national scale.
- **17.07.2026, 17:39** — the Cabinet, in agreement with the President, named **Yevhen Khmara acting Minister of Defence** and Andrii Sybiha acting Foreign Minister (LB). Fedorov had **declined** Zelensky's offer of an adviser post.
- **18.07.2026** — protests ran for a **third day**. The Kyiv action on Ivan Franko Square ran from ~15:00 and reached **«близько 2 тисяч людей»** by 21:45 (Suspilne, now reporting Kyiv directly), below the day-2 peak. **Mykolaiv grew to «понад 150 людей»** (from 45 on day 1, crossing into 100–999) and Kharkiv police counted **«близько 400»** at 20:00 — both wave highs for their cities. Solidarity rallies were also held **abroad** — Warsaw, Brussels, Sydney — outside the map's scope. Late on 18 July Zelensky held long talks with **Fedorov and Syrskyi** and announced a «рішення щодо армії» (a decision on the army) without reversing the reshuffle; Yevhen Khmara (acting SBU head) continues as acting Defence Minister. A Verkhovna Rada petition to keep Fedorov (author Svitlana Sirko, opened 16 July) is gathering signatures.
- **19.07.2026** — protests ran for a **fourth day** and drew the **largest crowd yet in Kyiv: «понад 5000 людей»** on Ivan Franko Square (Interfax-Ukraine via LIGA), above the day-2 peak. Actions continued in ~15 cities — Lviv, Dnipro, Kharkiv («понад 400»), Odesa, Mykolaiv, Ivano-Frankivsk («приблизно 250») and others — and reached **two cities new to the map, Mukachevo and Kamianske**. The Verkhovna Rada **went into recess until 18 August without appointing a defence minister** — Klymenko's candidacy was never submitted (Ivan Vyhivsky took the interior ministry) and **Yevhen Khmara remains acting**. The demand — Fedorov back, Syrskyi out — is unmet, so the protests continue.

**The finding of this run: the reshuffle was not reversed, and the protest outgrew it.** Fedorov was not reappointed; an acting minister, Khmara, runs the ministry while parliament is in recess to 18 August; and the movement is now in its **fourth day**, larger than ever (Kyiv 5000+), with a widened target (the Commander-in-Chief) and solidarity abroad. The map records **where** and **how large**, over 16–19 July; it does not adjudicate the political outcome.

## ⚠️ METHODOLOGICAL WARNINGS

**1. Suspilne's national round-up is a stale mirror of its own regional desks.** The text matches word for word; only the figure differs. As of 15:00 the round-up (updated 11:41) still showed Mykolaiv «26 людей» (regional desk: «45», 11:02) and Dnipro «близько 100» (regional desk: «близько 300», 15:00). **No figure from the round-up can be used without checking the regional desk.**

**2. Kharkiv inverts that pattern.** There the round-up gives more («щонайменше 300», unattributed) than the regional desk («близько 200», police spokesperson, 12:10). The direction is opposite to Mykolaiv and Dnipro, so "the regional desk is always higher" fails as a rule. Time resolves it: MediaPort on the scene watched the crowd grow from «близько 100» to «орієнтовно до 300» within half an hour, matching «щонайменше 300». The police figure of «близько 200» at 12:10 is a later snapshot, after part of the crowd had left. **The daily peak is about 300.**

**3. Search snippets fabricate figures.** Phrases recorded that **appear in no page body**: «кілька тисяч» (Kyiv), «близько тисячі» (Kharkiv), «близько 400 молодих людей» (Lviv). Snippets also credited an AFP quote to Reuters, and a Kyiv Post quote to AP. **Every figure here comes from the body of a page that was actually retrieved.**

**4. Headlines inflate.** TSN: headline «вийшли **тисячі людей**», body text «сотні» at most. Gal-Info: «**масштабна** акція», no figure anywhere in the body.

**5. Most "contradictions" are crowd growth, not conflicting sources.** Dnipro 100→300, Cherkasy 30→100, Khmelnytskyi 50→100, Kharkiv 100→300 within half an hour. Every figure is therefore given **with its time**.

**6. `dateModified` is not evidence of an update.** Ternopil, Zaporizhzhia and Chernivtsi carry fresh modification stamps (15:07–15:30) over text that is word-for-word the morning version: the CMS touched the page without a recount. Trust only markers inside the text, such as «ОНОВЛЕНО. 13:30».

**7. Telegram serves timestamps in UTC.** Kyiv is UTC+3. Zhelezniak's posts need +3 (anchored: «289» at 09:12 UTC = 12:13 Kyiv in LB). Carrying his timestamps over as Kyiv time shifts the chronology by three hours.

**8. Ukrainian inflects city names, so a nominative-case grep returns a false zero.** Searching «Кременчук» in KP.UA's text returns nothing, because the text reads «Кременчу**ці**». Same class of trap as encoding: **dumskaya.net and monitor.cn.ua serve windows-1251**, and reading them as UTF-8 also returns a false zero.

**9. An error in a source, left uncorrected:** Censor.NET's Mykolaiv section says Fedorov was dismissed «з посади **міністра цифрової трансформації**». This contradicts the rest of its own text and every other source. Recorded as published.

**10. The figure selection rule.** Take the figure that agrees across several independent sources, is the latest in time and the largest: the **daily peak**, not the count at a given hour. The aim is to estimate how many people turned out at maximum over the whole day. This follows from warning 5: crowds grew, so an early figure is not a smaller estimate but a different moment.

**11. The 19 July run (day 3) refined the «10 тис.» figure and reran discovery.** The day-2 evening «понад 10 тис.» in Kyiv is **not** snippet fabrication after all: Interfax-Ukraine reported it as a wire story (17 Jul, 18:28; retrieved here via Espreso's citation «за даними „Інтерфакс-Україна"»). It is still not used as the peak — LB's own correspondent on the scene gave «близько 3 тисяч», a ~3× gap that is flagged, not silently resolved — but it is a reported figure, not the fabrication class of day 1's «кілька тисяч». Aggregators (Telegraf, mignews) also circulated it. Separately, the v3 discovery method was rerun across the whole wave (gazetteer-grep over every downloaded round-up, batch tier-A queries, targeted city searches) and surfaced **no verifiable city beyond the 25**: the four cities a competitor's Telegram map flagged (Irpin, Boryspil, Kalush, Drohobych) appear in no round-up body and no city-specific report, and every gazetteer hit was a false positive — the organiser's surname «Козятинський», the President's name «Володимир», war-attack city lists (Ізюм, Марганець), a media brand («Еспресо.Біла Церква», which the outlet separately reported as *not* protesting), the ammunition-depot case «Вишневе». **A date trap persists:** several pieces search surfaces as «second/third day» are in fact dated a day earlier — read the article's own date before trusting a day label.
